# The Short Letter Costs More — purification IS the labor

**Goethe (via TRON):** he would have written a SHORT love letter, but he had no time — so it became LONG. **Short is HARDER than long, not easier.**

## Principle
- **Length is the DEFAULT** (accumulation, no labor). **Brevity is the ACHIEVEMENT** (distillation labor).
- **Purify = shorten under TWO hard constraints:** (1) **NO LOSS** of a critical step; (2) **CONSISTENT** with its governing law / heading / numbers.
- **The VERIFY is the second half of the work** — not a check on it. Reconcile the short form against the source AND the governing invariant before it is done.

## Why it matters (measured, this team)
- An **unrecallable** artifact is INERT: a 249-line prose skill even its keeper could not recall = not canon, dead on the page. Committed+readable is NOT enough — it must be **short enough to HOLD** (Phase-1 survival-checklist #2, `agent-rewind.md`).
- **Fast-shorten (cheap) fails BOTH constraints:** it drops the load-bearing step (a rewind-table that lost "the agent saves its own context") AND ships inconsistency (a 3-phase table under a 2-phase law).

## The move (CMM4 loop)
distill → **verify no-loss** (every critical rule still present, cited) → **verify consistent** (agrees with its law, the heading above it, the numbers elsewhere) → repeat.

## Applies to
every skill, essence, credo, anchor, report. Give each over-long artifact a **recallable head** (credo/table); demote detail to cited provenance. The keeper's job is not to KEEP the long letter — it is to PAY FOR the short one.
