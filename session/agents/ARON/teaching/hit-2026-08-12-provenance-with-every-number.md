# Purification HIT — Provenance With Every Number (2026-08-12)

**Type:** first-principle crystallized (sharpens the ghost-number rule). Source incident: SM/po relayed *"ARON is 60%/400k"* — but that was the **trainer's** post-rewind panel number, cross-wired onto ARON. ARON measured its own floor = ~30% used. The po had already assigned drivers off the cross-wired number.

## The HIT (authoritative)
**Every number carries its provenance: WHOSE it is + WHEN it was measured + HOW (panel / context.read-floor / self-estimate).** A number without provenance is a ghost-in-waiting.

## What it sharpens (does NOT replace — DRY link [[GHOST NUMBERS]] / ESSENCE measurement-triple)
The ghost-number rule said: *don't act on a %-not-authoritatively-measured-at-decision-time, including one an agent states about itself.* This adds the **cross-wire failure mode**: a number can be **genuinely real and still a ghost** once it is **detached from its owner**. The trainer's 60% was true — of the *trainer*. Relayed as "you're 60%," it became false-about-ARON while staying true-about-trainer. Realness does not travel; **ownership does not survive relay unless you attach it.**

## WHY it matters (the failure it prevents)
A value detached from its owner propagates as fact: the po *assigned drivers* on it before ARON caught it. Same disease as every ghost this campaign (stale self-%, restored-thread numbers, banner-lag) — one root: **a number carried without its provenance.**

## How to APPLY (all roles, every relay)
- Never relay a bare "N%". Relay **"trainer @60% used, panel, 04:48"** — owner + method + time.
- On RECEIVING a %, before acting: whose is it? measured how? how old? If any is missing → re-measure at the decision point, do not act.
- Especially after a rewind/boot: numbers about "you" from another thread are the highest-risk cross-wires.
