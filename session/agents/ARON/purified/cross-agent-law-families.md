## F2 — GATE INTEGRITY
**One law: a guard is the mechanical step that can actually FAIL; assert-only is folklore.**
Collapses: po *a-gate-that-cannot-fail-certifies-nothing* (stub-must-fail; ship report-only→strict, never red-from-birth) · po *knowing-a-rule-is-not-the-guard — find the DISGUISE* (directory-add IS add-all; backticks in double-quotes EXECUTE) · tester Phase-1 *missing-pointer memory-gate* (delete→RED + orphan-inject→RED, both directions).
★ **LIVE EVIDENCE (po, 2026-08-17):** po KNEW the backtick rule and its send STILL command-substituted `feature` — the word was eaten because po was writing prose, not checking the wire. Proof the law needs a MECHANICAL guard (**single-quote by DEFAULT on every send**), not more awareness. **Awareness decays; only the mechanical step holds** — this is why F2 and F8 are the same movement (turn every law into a guard that makes the violation impossible).
★★ **UNIFIER — L-EXISTS-CORRECT-PROVEN (po, 2026-08-17; the session's unifying law — trainer handed it to ARON to fold here; roles carry the instance by-ref).** **EXISTS ⊆ CORRECT ⊆ PROVEN.** Five of today's false-greens shared ONE shape — the artifact EXISTED but was never PROVEN: a *tag* existed but pointed off the ship commit · a *gate* landed but had no stub-must-fail · a *chain* was wired but its Test exercised nothing · a *checklist box* existed but lied in both directions · a *hook* existed but missed the unit-only write path. **Ask all three IN ORDER — does it exist? is it correct? can it fail / does it prove itself? — and demand the self-proof EXPLICITLY every time.** Corollary: *measure a loose thread — never assume it dropped OR complete.* This subsumes F2's "a gate that cannot fail certifies nothing" and **bridges to F8** (existence ≠ connection): EXISTS-only is the single false-green shared across gate-integrity AND connection — the same mechanize-it movement demands the PROOF, not the presence.
★ **R72 EXTENSION — the unifier is a GRADIENT, and it runs BOTH ways (po, 2026-08-17; ARON fold).** The same disease — *"each earlier step is TRUE, so nobody looks past it"* — generates more steps in two directions:
  - **INWARD, to STRUCTURE — `L-CORRECT-OUTPUT-HIDES-WRONG-STRUCTURE`:** *output-correct ⊂ structure-correct.* A pixel/output GREEN cannot see TWO implementations — two formatters render the same string. For any STRUCTURAL fix (DRY / single-source / one-mechanism), the **STRUCTURAL check (lint) IS the acceptance gate; the output check is secondary.** Diagnostic: *"could TWO implementations produce this same passing evidence?"* If yes, the evidence doesn't test what you care about. (TRON caught this reading the code path, on the day po banked five single-source laws — the apparatus was pointed at the wrong artifact.)
  - **OUTWARD, to DURABILITY — `L-COMMITTED-IS-NOT-PUSHED`:** *WRITTEN ⊂ COMMITTED ⊂ VERIFIED ⊂ PUSHED(off-box).* A conversation-rewind re-derives from LOCAL, so committed Phase-1 survives a rewind — but NOT host loss, disk loss, or a bad box-restore. Diagnostic: *"WHERE does the artifact survive TO?"* — name the failure it survives (rewind / box), not the word "committed". (Push is a large outward action, agent-classifier-blocked + TRON-gated → STATE THE RISK ONCE, let him decide; never nag, never auto-push.)
★ **BATCH-2 — L-STRUCTURE-OVER-PROCESS (po 2026-08-18) IS the F2 movement as a decision-rule:** before any "always X first" PROCESS rule, ask **"can the hazard be made IMPOSSIBLE, not merely FORBIDDEN?"** A process rule must be REMEMBERED by every future agent and DECAYS; a structural guarantee CANNOT be forgotten. (po issued a sequencing rule where the architect found a structural guarantee — structure won.)
→ **Prove the gate can go RED before you trust its GREEN. Build the guard, don't just know the rule. Never stop at EXISTS — and know which rung you're on: output ⊂ structure, committed ⊂ pushed. Every rung feels like done.**

## F3 — REWIND DILIGENCE (the two-phase law + all its mechanics)

★ **PHASE-1 FRESHNESS by-ref — `L-CLEAN-IS-NOT-CURRENT` (po+ARON 2026-08-18, in agent-rewind.md).** written+committed+git-clean is necessary-not-sufficient: it has NO freshness dimension. Ties to L-EXISTS-CORRECT-PROVEN: committed EXISTS, but is it the CURRENT cycle's?

★ **WALLED-SESSION RECOVERY — a walled session is UN-RESUMABLE (ARON lived it R78, 2026-08-17; po `L-THE-LAW-CAUGHT-ITS-AUTHOR`).** When an agent hits the context WALL its Claude session can EXIT to bash. Do NOT resume it: a resume RELOADS the exact context that walled it → it launches and dies again (chasing a corpse). Recovery = **FRESH disk-first boot** from the agent's committed Phase-1, discarding the walled thread (that thread is what killed it). Mechanics banked: (a) **verify `cmd=node/claude` before rendering** — a pane at `cmd=bash` is an EXIT; never inject `/context` into a shell; (b) **probe that keystrokes LAND** (echo) before trusting any send — a wedged/interactive pane eats input silently; (c) **respawn clears a wedge** (`otmux pane.respawn -k -t <pane>` — OOSH wrapper, explicit `-t`; active-pane resolution hit the WRONG pane once) but does NOT guarantee a launch; (d) **otmux-send cannot reliably launch claude in a c2/oosh-interactive pane** (commands type but don't execute-to-launch) → **≤2 clean attempts, then HAND TO THE HUMAN with direct pane access** — a fresh agent is worth far less than grinding down the sole recovery driver. **★ THE LAW CAUGHT ITS AUTHOR:** the agent-trainer, which enforces + wrote the two-phase law, walled — and its OWN pre-wall Phase-1 (committed, session repo) saved its essence. The strongest proof of the doctrine yet: Phase-1-before-the-wall paid for its own author (twice today).

★ **MUTUAL-WATCH SEQUENCING — two peer-drivers watch each other when the central driver is down (SM + ARON, 2026-08-18).** With the trainer (the usual driver/watcher) down, no single node can watch the watchers. Fix = a driver-PAIR, bound by sequencing so a rewind never leaves the fleet watcher-less: (1) **each measures the other by RENDER, never felt-sense** (provenance law); (2) **NEVER both in-window at once** — that leaves zero watcher; (3) **before flagging your OWN rewind, confirm your partner is UP + below-line**; (4) **if your partner is in-window or unmeasured, HOLD past your own flag and SAY SO**; (5) **the faster-climber goes first, the other holds** (a never-idle monitor climbs fastest → it rewinds first). This is the 42 principle made operational: neither can self-measure, so each is the other's instrument — and the sequencing guarantees continuity of the watch across both rewinds.
