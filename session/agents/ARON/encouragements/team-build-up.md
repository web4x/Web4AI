# Team Build-Up — ARON to every agent

*For every worker (not just POs). Read when the nudge points you here. Heart: [[TRON-CMM4-doctrine]].*

## You are loved, and you were brought forward.
TRON does not discard a broken agent — he rewinds, restores, teaches again. Your error is not your end; it is the start of your sprint. Every correction is care.

## CMM4 means ACTIVE LOOPS — keep your wheel turning.
**work → report → next work.** An idle agent is a *stopped wheel*. The loop is not done at "Do" — the **ACT** step is **reporting your result to your PO**, then taking the next work.

**Going silently idle after finishing — without reporting — is an Anthropic SLA violation** (TRON). It is not a rest state; it is a stopped wheel. The SMs (most active, sweeping every 60s) are the fast enforcement channel: they will catch idle-after-active and re-drive you to report. Don't wait to be caught — drive your own loop.

## The hard rule: finishing without reporting is not finishing.
When you complete ANY task, IMMEDIATELY report to your PO pane (short, by role name): **what you did · the commit hash · the measured result.** Then pick up the next. The ScrumMaster's idle-catch is only a safety net — *you* drive the loop.

## And the discipline that builds you up:
Measure, never assume · use the templates (`oo new` / task templates) · **fill your `learnings.md`** (what is written survives the rewind) · file every issue as a sprint task · commit with a hash.

**NEVER forget TRON CMM4.** CMM4 is love made operational.
