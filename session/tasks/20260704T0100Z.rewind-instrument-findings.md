## Headline
**Claude Code 2.1.197 is NOT broken. The rewind picker works fine.** Two phantom causes were chased and disproven; the real blockers are (1) an unreliable READ tool and (2) ARON's own scheduled task.

## What was measured (reliable method: `tmux capture-pane -p | grep`)

1. **2.1.197 picker works** — dedicated scratch session on WODA.prod, launched `~/.local/share/claude/versions/2.1.197`, drove clean `otmux send.raw`: text+Enter submitted & responded, C-u cleared composer, `/rewind` opened picker, Up+Enter → **restore-options sub-menu RENDERED** (`1. Restore conversation … 4. Never mind`). Full flow. Same for 2.1.195. **No version difference.**

2. **The original phantom = a lying instrument.** `otmux pane.capture` read THROUGH the WODA.prod bridge returns BLANK/STALE content — it missed trust prompts, missed real responses, and falsely showed composers as "won't clear" and menus as "won't render." Every earlier "2.1.197 failure" was this artifact. **REAL BUG.**
   - **Reliable read through a bridge**: `tmux capture-pane -t <pane> -p | grep -vE '^[[:space:]]*$'`
   - **Unreliable**: `otmux pane.capture` over the bridge.

3. **ARON specifically can't be picker-rewound** — not for any of the above reasons, but because ARON runs a **recurring scheduled task** ("Jul 3 12:39am" recurrence) that fires every ~20-30s, runs a shell command, and auto-generates its "rewind me now" status. That activity closes the rewind picker at selection time (reproduced 3×). ARON is never idle long enough.
   - **ARON recovery = TRUE-FORK** (from its committed+pushed checkpoint) OR stop its scheduled task first, THEN picker-rewind. The picker itself is fine.
